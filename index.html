ChessLens

Point your camera at a chessboard (a screen or a real board) and get the best move from Stockfish.

Everything runs on your phone. No video leaves the device.

…
Drag the four green dots onto the outer corners of the 8×8 squares (top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left as the camera sees it). The grid lines should sit on the squares. The small board above is what the app thinks it sees. If it looks wrong, adjust the corners or the lighting.
