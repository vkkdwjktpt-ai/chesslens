ChessLens

Point your camera at a chessboard (a screen or a real board) and get the best move from Stockfish.

Everything runs on your phone. No video leaves the device.

On-screen boards: the app finds the board by itself and follows the game move by move, so it only calculates when it is your turn. Hold the phone steady with the whole board in view. For true full screen on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from the icon.
Real boards (beta): fix the phone above or beside the board, turn Auto-find off, drag the four dots onto the board corners, set up the starting position, set the bottom colour, and tap Calibrate. The app then tracks every move from which squares empty and fill, so it works with any pieces. Promotions are assumed to be queens.
